Phone says "Your connection is not private"? Quick 5-second fix:

1 Apple iOS (Safari)

1. Tap Show Details at the bottom of the red/gray warning screen.
2. Tap "visit this website".
3. Tap "Visit Website" on the popup prompt. Your camera feed will start immediately!

2 Google Android (Chrome)

1. Tap Advanced on the "Your connection is not private" screen.
2. Tap "Proceed to 10.x.x.x (unsafe)".
3. Allow camera permissions when prompted.
